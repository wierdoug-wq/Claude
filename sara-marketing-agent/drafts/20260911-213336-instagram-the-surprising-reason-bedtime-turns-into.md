Bedtime isn't a battle because your kid is "difficult." Half the time, it's because their body isn't ready to power down yet — even if the clock says it should be.

Racing thoughts, wound-up energy, one more request for water... it's often a nervous system still running, not defiance.

A few things that can help:

→ Start winding down earlier than you think you need to
→ Keep the same 3–4 steps, same order, every night
→ Give a heads-up before each transition ("2 more minutes, then teeth")
→ Let them pick one small thing (which book, which pajamas)

Predictability calms a busy brain faster than reasoning with it does.

This works whether your kid has a diagnosis, is waiting on one, or just runs hot at night — all kids benefit from a nervous system that knows what's coming.
