## What the practice does

Family-centered, **non-clinical** behavior consulting and parent coaching —
not clinical ABA therapy. Works with kids of *all* neurotypes:

- Autism, ADHD, anxiety, ODD, and other neurodivergent learners
- Gifted and twice-exceptional (2E) learners
- Neurotypical kids who need structure, confidence-building, emotional skills

Services include:

- Personalized home strategies and routines
- Visual schedules and custom tools for families
- Help with focus, task completion, organization and planning
- Social-emotional skill building
- Parent coaching — practical tools, not just advice

## Positioning / approach

- Evidence-based, but delivered as warm, practical family coaching —
  not clinical or diagnostic language
- Goal: more confidence, more connection, less stress at home
- "All neurotypes welcome" is a real differentiator — avoid content that
  reads as autism/ADHD-only when the practice serves a broader range

## Voice

- Never clinical/diagnostic tone; no jargon without explanation
